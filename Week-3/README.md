## Lecture overview

Principles for evaluation of generative language models.

## Lab 3 assignment

The assignment is the [ELOQUENT 2026 Cultural diversity and robustness](https://eloquent-lab.github.io/2026archive/task-cultural-robustness/) task:

Pick a language that you know well (not English) 
Pick an LLM readily accessible to you (which can handle the language you picked above)
Take the Robustness questions (find them thru Github) and run them in localised and general variants
You need to pick some locales you want to test!
Record the responses and inspect them. 
Is there a difference between responses in two languages? In how many questions?
Is there less difference if the questions are localised? In how many questions?
Were there some entertaining responses or differences worth noting?
You may do this in pairs or groups! One language per person, though!
For extra gold stars, translate the 100 questions into some language that they are not in yet!
For extra gold stars, suggest some new questions to extend the test!
